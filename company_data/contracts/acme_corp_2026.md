### 6. Special Security, Compliance & Operational Requirements

* **Regulated Workload Scope:** Acme Corporation will process health administration transaction payloads containing Protected Health Information (PHI).

* **Compliance Frameworks:** BAA (Business Associate Agreement) executed concurrently under **HIPAA Compliance Guidelines**.

* **IP Whitelisting & Ingress Boundaries:** Strict IP restriction enforced. Tenant administrative interface access must be restricted exclusively to Acme corporate CIDR blocks (`192.0.2.0/24` and `198.51.100.0/24`).

* **Encryption Standards:** Full AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

* **Custom Log Streaming (Section 6.4):** All execution logs, API payload metadata, and tenant administrative audit trails **must be mirrored in real-time** to Acme’s external SIEM collector endpoint (`https://siem-collector.acmecorp.com/v1/ingest`) via HMAC-SHA256 signed headers **prior to live production Go-Live authorization**. Standard Northstar 90-day internal log retention alone does not satisfy this contractual obligation.
